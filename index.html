Who we are

Rêve d'Enfance is a student charity from HEC Paris. Every week, our volunteers visit children with cancer in hospital. Every month, we take them on outings. And every summer, we sail with them around Corsica, for free. Our goal is simple: help them forget the hospital, even for one day.

What we do

Hospital visits

Every week, we spend time with the children in their rooms: games, drawings and lots of laughs.

Monthly outings

Disneyland, the zoo, the Cité des Sciences, indoor surfing, a trampoline park... Once a month, we take the children out of the hospital.

Workshops

Pastry with the chefs of Lenôtre, baking for Christmas with l'Assauce, drawing and painting with the BDA: the children create and take home their work.

Corsica cruise

Our biggest project: every summer, a free sailing cruise around Corsica for the children.

Advent calendar

24 days, 8 activities and more than €5,000 raised for the children.

Christmas market

Cookies and plush toys on our campus stand, to fund the next adventures.
